## Hi, I'm Mehdi Sadat Hosseini

AI engineer with **6+ years** of building production computer vision systems for industry, and a **PhD student at Gdańsk University of Technology**, researching semantic segmentation under label noise in optical remote sensing.

### Impact in numbers

- **100+** industrial computer vision projects delivered (textile, automotive, glass, medical devices, packaging)
- **Over 99% uptime** for QC machines that have inspected **more than 1 billion goods**
- **20 inspection machines** in production with improved accuracy
- **250,000+ annotations** collected on the [ai-trainer.monitait.com](https://ai-trainer.monitait.com) platform
- **~90% less annotation effort** with a multi-modal auto-labeling pipeline (SAM + YOLOE + Qwen3)
- **mAP@0.5 above 92% at 60 fps** on edge devices, with class imbalance of about 1:500

### What I build

- **[Monitait.com](https://monitait.com)**: end-to-end platform for industrial visual inspection. Factory teams upload data, train defect detection models and deploy real-time inference to production lines, with MLOps pipelines simple enough for shop-floor workers.
- **Auto-labeling**: foundation models (SAM, YOLOE, Qwen3) for semi-automatic labeling of domain-specific imagery.
- **Research**: learning from noisy labels, class imbalance and spatially stratified evaluation.
